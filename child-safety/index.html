Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Developer: Melih Gökmen | Application: Deeper
Commitment to Child Safety

This document outlines the strict Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) prevention standards adhered to by developer Melih Gökmen for the mobile application Deeper and all related services, in full compliance with Google Play Child Safety Standards and international child protection laws.

1. Zero Tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

Deeper and developer Melih Gökmen enforce a strict, unwavering zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

We categorically forbid any user from posting, transmitting, requesting, sharing, storing, or promoting:

  • Any visual, audio, or written depictions of minors engaged in sexually suggestive or explicit conduct (CSAM).
  • Any behavior intended to groom, solicit, coerce, or sexually exploit children or minors (CSAE).
  • Child trafficking, sexual extortion (sextortion) targeting minors, or any predatory behavior endangering children.
Immediate Action on Violations

Any account, message, or content identified as involving CSAE/CSAM will result in immediate and permanent account termination, device-level banning, immediate deletion of offending media from our servers, and prompt reporting to law enforcement authorities.

2. Age Restriction & Protection of Minors

Deeper is strictly designed and intended for adults aged 18 and older:

  • Minors (under the age of 18) are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, accessing, or using the application.
  • Age verification measures and user onboarding agreements explicitly state that anyone under 18 may not participate.
  • Any user profile found to belong to a minor, or depicting a minor, is immediately removed upon discovery.

3. In-App Reporting & Moderation Mechanisms

To ensure user safety and swift moderation, Deeper provides accessible in-app mechanisms:

  • In-App Profile Reporting: Users can flag suspicious or violating user profiles directly within the app using the "Report" button on every profile page.
  • In-App Chat Reporting: Users can report harmful or suspicious messages or interactions directly inside the chat interface.
  • Block Mechanism: Users can instantly block any individual to cease all communications and hide content.
  • Direct Support Channel: Users may also submit reports directly via email or our support center. All child safety reports are prioritized with urgent response protocols.

4. Cooperation with Law Enforcement & NCMEC

In accordance with global legal frameworks and Google Play requirements, we cooperate fully with relevant international and national child safety authorities:

  • Any substantiated reports or discoveries of CSAM are reported immediately to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and competent law enforcement agencies.
  • We preserve audit trails and necessary diagnostic records in compliance with legal warrants and child safety reporting guidelines.
